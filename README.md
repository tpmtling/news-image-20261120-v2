A classic newspaper front page that sums up Friday 20 November 2026 in one 1024x1024 image: the World Court's historic ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya leads, with Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast, the Gulf war widening around Riyadh and Hormuz, the Trump-Xi-Putin peace declaration from Shenzhen, and Anthropic's $83 IPO pricing, plus a five-item briefs strip. The picture is drawn deterministically with Pillow by `render.py` from the hand-picked stories in `news_data.md`; `main.py` (standard library only) copies the finished `news_image.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
